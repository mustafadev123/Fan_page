About Real Madrid

Real Madrid is a professional football club from Madrid, Spain. The club was founded in 1902 and is known around the world for its white kit, proud history, and tradition of competing for the biggest trophies.

The Santiago Bernabeu is the club's famous home stadium. Real Madrid has had many legendary players and has created unforgettable moments in Spanish and European football.

My Love for the Club

I have been supporting Real Madrid and football in general since 2013. Since then, the club has become a very important part of my life. I follow the matches, celebrate the victories, and stay loyal during the difficult moments too.

I consider myself a diehard Real Madrid fan. In my room, I have posters of Gareth Bale, Cristiano Ronaldo, and Karim Benzema wearing the Real Madrid jersey. I also have a poster of the legendary coach Zinedine Zidane. These players and this coach represent an unforgettable era for the club and are a big part of why my support is so strong.
